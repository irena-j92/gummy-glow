# Header gummy and review paper assets

Created with built-in imagegen on 2026-10-14; originals retained. Sharp was used only for format conversion/downscaling. Both assets are stored inside the Site repository and deployed locally with it.

- `public/assets/optimized/hero-pink-gummy.webp`: 640×632 RGBA, 53,102 bytes. Target was the original pink swirl gummy; color reference was the original watermelon pouch. Transparency was verified.
- `public/assets/review-paper-texture.webp`: 768×768 RGB, 105,844 bytes. White fibers are blended over white at 50% opacity. Seamless tiling was requested; a faint tile boundary may remain under close inspection.

## Final gummy prompt

Recolor only the supplied isolated six-lobed spiral gummy to the lighter pastel magenta/lilac pink of the watermelon pouch, approximately #FBA2F3. Preserve its exact shape, outline, orientation, spiral direction, framing, material, shading, and highlights. Keep one isolated gummy with genuinely transparent exterior. No pouch, text, extra objects, glow, shadow, or backdrop.

## Final paper prompt

Create a square seamless white uncoated paper texture, macro fiber and micrograin only. Base #FCFCFB with extremely subtle low-contrast light-gray cellulose fibers. Flat even diffuse lighting; seamless repeating edges. No objects, shadows, folds, creases, scratches, stains, writing, borders, vignette, or directional gradients. Full-bleed opaque image.
